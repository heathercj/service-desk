## Symptoms

The VPN client shows "authentication failed" or spins indefinitely when
connecting.

## Resolution

1. Sign out of the VPN client completely.
2. Clear the cached credential profile (Settings > Accounts > Forget this
   device).
3. Reinstall the connection profile from the company portal.
4. Reconnect and sign in again.

## Verification

Confirm you can reach an internal-only resource (e.g. the intranet home
page).
